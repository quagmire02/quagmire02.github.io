View 01 About

Every video is a 3D scene in disguise.

I'm Wasif, a computer vision researcher in Dhaka working on spatial AI: getting machines to understand the 3D world from ordinary cameras.

Most of my work is built on feed-forward 3D models like MapAnything and VGGT, which recover camera poses and depth from plain video in a single pass. I make them fit on small GPUs and turn what they see into point clouds, Gaussian splats and digital twins you can fly through, or drop into Unreal Engine.

On the robotics side, I was research head of BRACU Duburi's AI and machine vision team, working with them on how our underwater robots see: auto-labelled training data, perception running live on a Jetson Orin, and testing in simulation before anything touched water. Lately I pair vision-language models with geometry, so they measure the world instead of guessing at it.
